Description

You have a number of bales of hay.  For some reason, instead of being weighed individually, they were weighed in all possible combinations of two. The weights of each of these combinations were written down and arranged in numerical order, without keeping track of which weight matched which pair of bales. The weights are given in a list.  Print the weight of each bale (bales) as a list of integers.
