Equal-sized groups: the 1000-element instance did not finish with the direct encoding (no proof in 60 s or 120 s, millions of nodes). Restricting each break point to the admissible positions (after the last element of a run of equal values, a set letting plus x in toSet) made the proof finish in about 7.5M nodes. Accepted on all 5 instances, with 180 s per instance, but the 1000-element case needs most of that budget.
