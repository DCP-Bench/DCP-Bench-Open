Warehouses (cities) open or closed; each open warehouse ships up to max_shipping units in total; each region's demand is met exactly.
- open_warehouse[i] = 1 if warehouse i is opened -> BoolVarArray open(*this, n, 0, 1)
- if New York is opened, Los Angeles must also be opened -> rel(*this, open[NY], IRT_LQ, open[LA])
- no more than three warehouses operational in any week -> linear(*this, open, IRT_LQ, 3)
- either Atlanta or Los Angeles must be opened -> rel(*this, open[LA], BOT_OR, open[ATL], 1)
- shipments from a warehouse only when it is open, and it ships at most max_shipping in total -> sum_j ships[i][j] <= max_shipping * o_i, where o_i is open[i] as IntVar via channel; linear with coefficients {1,..,1,-max_shipping} (not a row-sum-only global; no global row fits)
- each region's demand is met exactly by the warehouses -> linear(col_j, IRT_EQ, demands[j]) over the column j of ships
- ships[i][j] in 0..min(demands[j], max_shipping) (bound from the instance)
- total cost = fixed costs of the opened warehouses + shipping costs -> linear(a, vars, IRT_EQ, total), total in 0..sum of max costs (bound from instance data)
- branching: open first (BOOL_VAR_NONE, BOOL_VAL_MIN), then ships (INT_VAR_SIZE_MIN, INT_VAL_MIN)
- objective: minimize total
