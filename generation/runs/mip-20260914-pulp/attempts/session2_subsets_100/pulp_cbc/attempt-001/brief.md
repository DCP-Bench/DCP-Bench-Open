# session2_subsets_100
Instance: `A`. Outputs: `in_S` and `in_T`, one 0/1 per element.
Two disjoint non-empty subsets with equal sums. The reference writes disjointness
as sum(in_S * in_T) == 0; over binaries that is exactly "at most one of the two
per element", which is what a linear model can state.
