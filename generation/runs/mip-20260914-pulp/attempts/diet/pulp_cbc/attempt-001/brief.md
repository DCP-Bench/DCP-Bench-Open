# diet
Instance: `n`, `price`, `limits`. Output: `cost` only.
Buy integer quantities of n foods meeting four nutrient minima at least cost.
The nutrient table (calories, chocolate, sugar, fat per food) is a constant of
the problem in the reference, not instance data, and is mirrored as written.
Bounds are the reference's: quantities 0..10000, cost 0..1000.
