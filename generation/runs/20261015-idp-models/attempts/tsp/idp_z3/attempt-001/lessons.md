Rounded Euclidean distance has no exact linear form: r is pinned by r*(r-1) =< d2 =< r*(r+1) on an unbounded Int, and z3 cannot prove the optimum for 12 cities within 180 s.
Dropping the rounding (squared distance as cost) proves the 12-city optimum in about 12 s, so the Boolean leg and position encoding is not the bottleneck; the nonlinear rounding is.
Type ranges can only be sized from data by a dimension, or by ${} over a 1-D integer field; a 2-D coordinate array gives no range for a distance type.
